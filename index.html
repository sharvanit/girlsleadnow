What is Girls Lead Now?

Creating opportunities for girls to lead, connect, and make an impact in their communities.

Leadership starts with opportunity.

Girls Lead Now is a student-led organization dedicated to empowering girls through leadership, education, service, advocacy, and community engagement.

Through official chapters, members have the opportunity to develop leadership skills, work alongside other students, serve their communities, and turn their ideas into action.

Our Mission

To empower girls to become confident leaders, engaged community members, and changemakers by creating meaningful opportunities for leadership, education, service, advocacy, and connection.

What We Stand For

Our organization is built around creating meaningful opportunities for girls to lead and make a difference.

🌱

Leadership

Developing confident leaders who are ready to take initiative and create positive change.

📚

Education

Creating opportunities for girls to learn, explore new ideas, and grow their skills.

🤝

Community

Connecting girls through service, collaboration, advocacy, and shared experiences.
